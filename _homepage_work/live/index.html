FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

How does the free 24-hour subdomain work?

You get a yourname.falcon.codes URL for 24 hours to test the editor and themes. After that we delete it unless you upgrade to a paid plan.

How do I connect my own domain?

Add a CNAME record for www pointing to connect.falcon.codes, plus a TXT record to verify ownership. Click verify and we provision HTTPS automatically.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your dashboard at any time. Your site stays live until the end of the current billing period.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. A short wizard collects your business details and builds a polished starter site. On the Pro plan you can also edit HTML and CSS directly.
